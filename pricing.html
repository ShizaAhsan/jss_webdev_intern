Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our membership plans.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time with just one click. No questions asked, no hidden fees.

What's included in the Free plan?

The Free plan gives you 3 months of access to IT Support Training, Systems Admin fundamentals, networking basics, cloud fundamentals, and the JSS community.

Do I need my own cloud account?

For some cloud-specific courses, you'll need your own AWS or Azure account. However, we provide basic-level cloud subscriptions for introductory courses.

How does the yearly plan save me money?

The yearly plan costs $549/year vs $600/year ($50×12 months), saving you $51 annually — an 8% discount plus exclusive perks like priority support and resume review.
