About This Course

The Modern IT Support Training: 220-1201 (Core 1) course is designed to equip you with the foundational skills needed for a successful career in IT support. This comprehensive program focuses on practical, real-world skills, offering insights into networking, troubleshooting, and mobile device management.

This course emphasizes skills development and real-world application, allowing you to confidently handle IT roles. From configuring IP addresses to understanding mobile connectivity, every lesson is tailored to provide you with the practical expertise needed in the IT industry.

What You'll Learn

  • Identify key components of laptop hardware.
  • Understand basics of network configurations and protocols.
  • Recognize the roles of IP addressing and subnetting.
  • Apply effective troubleshooting techniques in IT support.
  • Mobile Device Support & Connectivity.
  • Prepare for entry-level IT roles.
